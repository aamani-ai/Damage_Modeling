# Solar-wind normalized-response comparison - assumptions and watchlist

| ID | Assumption or watch item | Current treatment | Change trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| SWNR-A01 | Exact numerical equality across the compared synthetic records is intentional | Verify identity as an audit fingerprint after each cell-local adoption | A cell records an evidence-backed or owner-authorized cell-local divergence |
| SWNR-A02 | The current profile is solar-specific despite being hazard-label-neutral | Limit comparison to the four declared fixed/tracker PV subjects | A separately governed profile is proposed for another asset class |
| SWNR-A03 | Strong-wind origin does not provide TC transfer evidence | Keep every TC parameter Tier 4 and cell-local | A qualified cross-hazard transfer study passes independent review |
| SWNR-A04 | Candidate v0.1 is not a runtime dependency | Store only comparison identity/path/SHA metadata with `runtime_dependency: false` | A new shared-runtime proposal passes the shared-component, cell-promotion, and consumer gates |
| SWNR-A05 | Cell axes and selectors can differ even when the synthetic response payload matches | Compare only the declared parameter fingerprint; leave hazard bridges and contracts cell-local | A governed shared axis/selector contract is separately approved |

## Watchlist

- Do not call the profile empirical, calibrated, conservative, or probabilistically weighted.
- Do not let byte equality substitute for TC occurrence, architecture/state, severe-tail, disposition/cost,
  value, or independent-validation evidence.
- Do not load the candidate into a curve artifact or count it as canonical runtime coverage.
- Do not make a TC cell depend on the profile's availability or version at evaluation time.
- Do not apply the solar response to GSU/substation or other withheld units.
- Do not interpret a later cell-local divergence as an error when its authority and version change are
  explicitly governed.
