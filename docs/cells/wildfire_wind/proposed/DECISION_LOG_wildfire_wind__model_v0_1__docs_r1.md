# Decision log — wildfire_wind model v0.1/docs r1

## WW-D001 — New cell closes as v0.1, not a screening v1

No reviewed public chain supports an external-wildfire economic DR. Coverage is established with a zero-curve
package; a Tier-4 screening table would require a later explicit business decision and semantic version.

## WW-D002 — External and endogenous fire remain separate

Internal electrical/mechanical/maintenance/lightning fires have different causes and frequencies. They may
inform response anatomy but cannot calibrate exogenous-wildfire vulnerability.

## WW-D003 — Thermal and firebrand pathways remain separate

Uniform or local flux/contact histories and particle deposition/ingress histories require different axes and
tests. Consequential internal spread is a dependent state after ignition, not an additive event.

## WW-D004 — Turbine damage is one dependency-safe repeated assembly

Rotor, nacelle, tower shell/base, and internal services remain component zones, but one turbine owns the
mutually exclusive economic state. This prevents a terminal fire from charging multiple replacement curves.

## WW-D005 — GSU apparatus is decomposed and valued once

Transformer, switchgear/bus, protection/control/DC, and terminations have different exposure and disposition.
Their anatomy can be asset-neutral, but ownership, local wildfire attack, value, capability, and release stay
in this cell.

## WW-D006 — FSim semantics transfer; solar ordinates do not

The wildfire source and site-field vocabulary are reusable. Solar module/electrical/civil ordinates and
aggregate loss weights are asset-specific Tier-4 screening judgments and cannot seed wind response.

## WW-D007 — Legacy three-curve package is rejected

The rotor/nacelle/tower logistics use a fixed-distance heat proxy, order-of-magnitude height attenuation,
invented ignition probabilities/caps, wrong-direction internal-fire cases, omit BOP, and conflict with their
own displayed tables. They remain negative regression fixtures only.

## WW-D008 — FM/AFAC/CFA controls are fields, not credits

Clearance, vegetation, access, shutdown, construction, and suppression guidance becomes auditable selector or
conditioner data. It does not create a numerical damage reduction.
