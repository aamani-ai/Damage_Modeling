# Pressure test — wildfire_wind model v0.1/docs r1

## Tests performed

### 1. Endpoint chain

```text
FSim/fireline source fields                         available
target-local thermal/firebrand/residue attack       partial physics; no qualified generic bridge
representative inspected failure-unit disposition   not located
same-unit direct repair/replacement cost ratio       not matched
site geometry, ownership, value, support allocation not complete
```

Outcome: withhold.

### 2. Solar-response substitution

The neighboring solar package shares FSim semantics and governance structure. Its module, electrical, and
civil state ordinates, vulnerability ordering, weights, caps, and value shares are target-specific Tier-4
screening judgments. Wind turbines are elevated composite/electromechanical assemblies; collection and GSU
apparatus have distinct geometry and replacement grain. Substitution is rejected.

### 3. Fireline-intensity substitution

Fireline intensity in kW/m cannot be used directly as equipment incident flux in kW/m². A fixed distance
and scalar attenuation omit flame geometry, radiation view, shielding, convection, flame contact, target
height/orientation, and duration. The legacy conversion is rejected.

### 4. Blade-specimen-to-economic substitution

The 15–75 kW/m² cone tests establish material mechanisms for specific GFR-UPR specimens. They do not supply
full-blade field loading, a representative turbine population, structural disposition, repair probability,
or direct cost. Curve knots, thresholds, and DR derived from that range are rejected.

### 5. Firebrand-study-to-ignition substitution

NIST individual/pile studies identify particle, wind, substrate, contact, deposition, and accumulation
variables. They do not establish turbine/GSU ingress, equipment ignition probability, dependent fire
spread, or cost. Equating short firebrand peaks with cone flux or generic ignition is rejected.

### 6. Internal-fire incident substitution

Internal, lightning, electrical, or maintenance fires can reveal turbine zones and dependent disposition
after ignition. They do not supply the probability that an external wildfire delivers or initiates that
state. Using them as exogenous-wildfire calibration is rejected.

### 7. Compliance and protection credits

AFAC, CFA, FM, and DNV establish useful control fields. Clearance, access, water, shutdown procedures,
containment, detection, suppression, or certification do not carry universal efficacy distributions. An
unknown or nominally compliant control receives no numerical credit.

### 8. Dependency-safe turbine state

One external ignition may damage blades, nacelle, and upper tower before one terminal repair/replacement
decision. Independent additive rotor/nacelle/tower curves can exceed the true same-turbine cost scope. The
v0.1 assembly unit avoids this error, but state probabilities and costs remain withheld.

### 9. GSU and shared-system test

The shared GSU appears once. Main transformer, switchgear/bus, protection-control/DC, and terminations are
separate addressable units within that shared yard. The 72 USD/kW aggregate electrical row cannot be
duplicated, labeled entirely as GSU, or split evenly. Unknown apparatus value withholds scenario loss.

### 10. Dollar denominator checks

```text
turbine equipment / physical = 1090 / 1623 = 67.159581%
turbine equipment / installed = 1090 / 1968 = 55.386179%
physical + excluded = 1623 + 345 = 1968  2023 USD/kW
```

These calculations test value anatomy only. They are not DR caps, exposure fractions, or a site appraisal.

### 11. Geometry checks

One turbine, collection segment, or GSU-yard intersection does not imply full-farm exposure. A lease or
permitted polygon is not an equipment footprint. Point, rotor/zone, line, yard, building, foundation, and
civil geometries remain separate. Unknown geometry withholds rather than defaulting to one.

### 12. Support double count

Fieldwork and transport/logistics total 294 USD/kW in the reference ledger. They are allocated once after
direct unit disposition. Giving them an intrinsic wildfire DR and scaling them again is prohibited.

### 13. Residue and disruption boundary

Conductive/corrosive destructive residue may become a physical pathway only with inspected equipment state
and cost. Generic smoke/ash cleaning, odor, downtime, derating, lost generation, and telemetry loss are not
direct destruction. Moving those consequences into the curve is rejected.

### 14. Zero and missing-evidence checks

No verified local attack does not authorize a published numerical zero from this package because no
runtime response exists. Foundation, buried collection, steel shells, or protected equipment are not
declared immune. Missing response remains withheld.

## Outcome

Every shortcut changes the source semantics, pathway, target population, failure unit, spatial grain,
disposition, temporal scope, or value denominator. The defensible model-v0.1 result is:

```yaml
curve_records: []
canonical_runtime_artifact: false
standard_reason: NO_RUNTIME_CURVE
```
