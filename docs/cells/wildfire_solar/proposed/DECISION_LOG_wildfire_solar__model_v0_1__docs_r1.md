# Decision log — wildfire_solar proposed model v0.1

Decision date: 2026-07-09. Scope: working research scaffold; no runtime curve.

| ID | Decision | Basis | Consequence | Revisit trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WS-D01 | Scope only exogenous burnover physical damage. | Pathway and denominator separation. | Smoke/ash production effects, PSPS, BI, equipment-origin fire, BESS, and financial terms remain outside this cell. | Combined-peril architecture review. |
| WS-D02 | Accept only the six canonical class IDs mapped exactly to FSim FIL1–FIL6 / FLP1–FLP6 as Gen-1 upstream hazard input. | `USFS_FSIM_RDS_2023`. | No continuous midpoint, reconstructed FLI band, or FIL6 cap. | Approved product-specific conversion or local exposure model. |
| WS-D03 | Keep burn probability outside M3. | Frequency/damage separation; `USFS_FSIM_RDS_2023` and `FINNEY_2011_FSIM`. | Damage, if later implemented, is conditional on the delivered exposure state. | Architecture change. |
| WS-D04 | Do not precommit a curve count. Maintain a candidate failure-unit inventory and require complete value/exposure coverage before any curve release. | Seven-step audit and value crosswalk. | No opaque whole-plant curve and no stale assumption of five curves plus a near-zero foundation curve. | Reviewed failure-unit/value architecture. |
| WS-D05 | Withdraw every initial FIL-to-DR ordinate and band. | No direct calibration supported any of the 90 values. | `curve_records` remains empty; all numerical outputs are withheld. | PV-specific calibration or documented structured expert elicitation. |
| WS-D06 | Reject the legacy `q = 0.35 I/d`, fixed 10 m distance, logistic curves, component weights, event anchors, and uncertainty bands. | Reproducible legacy audit and missing provenance. | Legacy material is source-discovery input only and has no runtime effect. | Independent evidence with exact endpoint, locator, units, and applicability domain. |
| WS-D07 | Separate burned fraction, attack fraction given burned, at-risk fraction, and conditional severity by component zone. | NIST exposure/vulnerability separation and value allocation contract. | A burned landscape pixel cannot silently imply whole-site attack. | Validated spatial exposure adapter. |
| WS-D08 | Define the future y-axis as conditional expected direct replacement-cost ratio of the same failure unit, given delivered local exposure and BOM state. | Governance/value-boundary review. | Support costs, BI, financial terms, and whole-plant insured loss are excluded from the curve ordinate. | Reviewed runtime model or denominator change. |
| WS-D09 | Allocate replacement fieldwork/logistics in rows 12, 13, and 15 once after damaged units are known; split mixed civil row 14 into direct failure-unit value versus support/pathway treatment first. | `VALUE_CROSSWALK` rows 12–15 and double-counting guardrail. | Support costs receive no independent fragility curve; eligible row-14 civil assets are not silently discarded or treated as support. | Claims-supported allocation rule and reviewed civil-asset split. |
| WS-D10 | Treat fences, walls, firebreaks, vegetation, burial, enclosures, access, and suppression as selectors/conditioners/exposure inputs—not generic discounts. | `DOE_FEMP_PV_WILDFIRE`, `NIST_TN_1796`, `NIST_TN_2205`, `NIST_TN_2228`, `COHEN_USDA_2000`, `ENERGY_SAFE_VICTORIA_SOLAR`, and `NSW_RFS_OP_1_2_22`. | Unknown controls receive no mitigation credit; load-bearing unknown exposure withholds loss. | Qualified and validated site-transfer model. |
| WS-D11 | Keep ember/firebrand attack as a separately governed pathway. | `NIST_TN_1796`, `NIST_TN_2205`, and `NIST_TN_2228`. | Ember fields may be captured, but they cannot emit damage in this scaffold or be folded into a radiant-flux curve. | Recipient-side PV ignition and exposure calibration. |
| WS-D12 | Do not treat low/high sensitivity anchors as a probability distribution. | No sample, elicitation protocol, distribution, or coverage calibration exists. | No PML, VaR, TVaR, tail spread, or confidence claim. | Reviewed uncertainty model. |
| WS-D13 | Use the NREL-derived workbook only as a generic reference-value archetype. | `VALUE_SOLAR_WORKBOOK` and `NREL_NLR_Q1_2025_PV_BENCHMARK`. | A site appraisal and component-zone inventory override the reference basis. | Benchmark vintage change or site valuation. |
| WS-D14 | Fail closed at every missing load-bearing seam. | Evidence, value, exposure, and reportability gates. | No numeric DR/loss/EAL is emitted; standard runtime reason is `NO_RUNTIME_CURVE`. | All release gates pass. |

The row-level evidence and parameter decisions are controlled by `CLAIM_PARAMETER_REGISTER_wildfire_solar__model_v0_1__docs_r1.csv` and `PARAMETER_TIER_TABLE_wildfire_solar__model_v0_1__docs_r1.csv`.
