# Bounded evidence search log — flood_wind model v0.1 / docs r1

## Question and stopping rule

Question: can public evidence support a representative wind-farm electrical flood curve whose ordinate is
same-unit direct repair or replacement cost ratio?

The search stopped after authoritative equipment guidance, utility-event lessons, government flood method
guidance, renewable asset anatomy, wind cost references, the canonical flood-solar cell, and the local Hazard
implementation had been reviewed and none closed the full chain:

    component-local flood state
      -> identified equipment and condition
      -> inspected disposition
      -> direct cost for the same replacement unit
      -> representative target population

## Endpoint assessment

NERC's 2022 relay-room event is material because shallow water at low-voltage control connections caused
large operational consequences. It does not report a same-unit repair-cost denominator. NEMA guidance can
support replacement/reconditioning categories, but it explicitly does not provide the population-calibrated
probability and cost chain needed for a DR curve. The public cost reference aggregates collection and
substation value.

## Bounded conclusion

No public matched chain was located within the surfaces above. This is a bounded negative result, not a claim
that private claims, OEM repair records, insurer engineering files, non-English sources, or later publications
do not exist.

## Refresh triggers

- component-level property claims with water depth/elevation, duration, contamination, energized state,
  disposition, and direct cost;
- OEM or utility post-event records with a same-unit replacement denominator;
- a public substation flood dataset with equipment construction and cost;
- a governed structured-elicitation study at the intended failure-unit grain;
- material new evidence in the canonical flood-solar cell.
